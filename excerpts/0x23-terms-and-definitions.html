SKELETON — to be authored by the working group.

Terms

For the purposes of this Standard, the following terms and definitions apply:

Authentication (authn)

process of verifying the identity of a principal

Authorization (authz)

process of determining what a verified principal is permitted to do

Collection

versioned set of TEA Artefacts associated with a specific TEA Release

Product

item sold, distributed or otherwise delivered under one name

Product Release

specific version of a Product that can be acquired or downloaded

TEA Artefact

named file (e.g. an SBOM, an attestation, a VEX document) referenced by a Collection
